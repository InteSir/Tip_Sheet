About The Tip Sheet

The Tip Sheet is a nail inspiration magazine with one simple idea: every look you love should come with a way to wear it.

Scroll Pinterest for nail ideas and you'll find thousands of beautiful manicures, but rarely a way to get the look without a salon appointment. So for every design we feature, we point you to a press-on set in the same shape, length and colour family.

What you'll find here

  • Nail ideas by season, occasion and trend, from Christmas red to New Year's champagne.
  • Browse filters so you can find designs by shape, length, colour and style.
  • Press-on buying guides that explain what to look for, not just what to buy.
  • How-to guides for application, wear and safe removal.
  • A small Holiday Home corner with cozy small-space decor ideas.
