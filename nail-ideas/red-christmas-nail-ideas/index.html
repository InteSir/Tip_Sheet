FAQ

What shade of red is best for Christmas nails?

Blue-based reds (cherry, crimson) feel the most classic and festive. Warmer orange-reds look brighter and more casual. Deep wine and burgundy reds work well if you want your nails to carry through January.

Are press-on nails a good option for Christmas nail art?

Yes. Detailed designs like bows, stripes and foil accents are often easier to get as a press-on set than to paint yourself, and you can swap them for a New Year's look the following week.

Which nail shape suits red nails?

Almond and oval shapes make red nails look elegant and lengthen the fingers. Short squoval or square shapes look neat and modern, especially for bold colour mixes.

How long before Christmas should I get my nails done?

If you are wearing press-ons, apply them one to two days before your main event so they have settled. See our guide on making press-on nails last for prep tips.
