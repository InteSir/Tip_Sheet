FAQ

What are coquette nails?

Coquette nails use soft, romantic details: sheer pinks and nudes, bows, pearls, lace-like lines and delicate French tips.

Are 3D bow press-on nails practical?

They are best on accent nails rather than every finger, so everyday tasks stay comfortable. Flat painted bows are a lower-profile alternative.

Which shape suits coquette nails?

Almond is the classic choice, and coffin works well for longer sets with more room for detail.
