FAQ

Can short nails look festive?

Yes. Short nails suit accent-nail designs, striped tips and metallic finishes particularly well because the details stay crisp and in proportion.

What is the best shape for short Christmas nails?

Squoval and round are the most practical and natural-looking. Short square shows off French tips and straight stripes.

Do press-on nails come in short sizes?

Many press-on sets are sold in short lengths. Check the listing for the length and the number of sizes in the box so you can match each nail.
