The Tip Sheet is an independent nail inspiration and press-on advice site. Here's how we put each page together, and what we'll never do.

## How we choose looks

- **Genuinely different ideas.** We'd rather show 20 distinct looks than pad a list to 25. Every look gets its shape, length, finish, approximate shades and a difficulty level, so you can tell at a glance whether it suits you.
- **Three honest routes.** Each look explains how to get it as a press-on, as a DIY manicure or at a salon, and says plainly when a route is hard or a ready-made set is difficult to find.
- **Trends with a source.** When we say a look is trending, we name where the signal comes from (for example, Pinterest's own trend reports) and what it measures. Search growth isn't the same as saves or sales, and we don't blur the two.

## How we write advice

- **Prep and fit first.** Most press-on problems come down to prep and sizing. Where brands or experts disagree, we show both sides instead of picking one to sound certain.
- **Safety is sourced.** Information about glue, gel and allergies comes from dermatology and public-health sources, and is never presented as medical advice. If something hurts, burns or itches, stop and speak to a doctor or dermatologist.
- **No invented experience.** We don't claim to have tested anything we haven't, and we don't invent wear times, ratings or reviews.

## Products and links

The Tip Sheet doesn't recommend or link to specific products at the moment, and has no affiliate links. If that changes, we'll explain how we choose products on this page, label every affiliate link, and update our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Images

Nail inspiration images marked "AI-generated inspiration" are illustrations of a design idea, never photos of a product. We don't make AI images of licensed characters or copy another artist's signature work. [How we use AI images](/how-we-use-ai-images/).

## Corrections

We update articles when advice changes, and each article shows when it was last updated. Spot a mistake? [Let us know](/contact/).
