**The Tip Sheet** is a nail inspiration and press-on advice site. We bring together three things that usually live in different places: distinctive nail ideas, honest instructions for getting each look, and practical answers when press-ons won't behave.

Scroll Pinterest for nail ideas and you'll find thousands of beautiful manicures, but rarely the details you need to recreate one. So every look here comes with its shape, length, finish and approximate shades, plus how to get it as a press-on, as a DIY manicure or at a salon.

## What you'll find here

- **Nail Ideas:** seasonal and trend-led edits, from red Christmas nails to New Year's champagne and chrome.
- **Browse:** find looks by shape, length, color, finish and occasion.
- **The Press-On Hub:** the Problem Solver for lifting and pop-offs, a sizing guide with a printable chart, a prep checklist, and how to choose a set.
- **Home & Decor:** a small corner of renter-friendly, small-space decor in the same palettes as our nail edits.
