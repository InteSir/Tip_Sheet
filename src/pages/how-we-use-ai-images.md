Some nail photos on The Tip Sheet are **AI-generated inspiration images**. We use them to show a design idea (a shape, a finish, a color combination) clearly and consistently, without copying another creator's work.

## Our rules

1. **Always labelled.** Every AI image that appears with a look carries the caption "AI-generated inspiration, not a product photo".
2. **Never a product photo.** An AI image shows an idea, not a product, and we never present one as what a set you can buy looks like. We don't edit or "improve" retailer photos with AI.
3. **Real routes, not promises.** Because the image is an idea, each look explains how to get it as a press-on, DIY or at a salon, and says when that's hard.
4. **No copied designs or characters.** We don't generate images of licensed characters or recreate another artist's signature work.
5. **Pinterest graphics.** Our Pins are built from our own AI inspiration images with our own typography and layout, and are marked as AI-generated where Pinterest asks for it.

## Real photos

The site doesn't show product photos at the moment. If we ever add them, they will come only from the brand or retailer with permission, or through an official retailer image tool we are approved to use. Photos from customer reviews are not ours to reuse.
