This policy explains what information The Tip Sheet ("we", "us") collects when you visit {{ site.url }} and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- **Contact form.** If you send us a message, we receive the name, email address and message you provide. The form is processed by our host, Netlify, and we use these details only to reply to you.
- **Server logs.** Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.
- **Analytics.** We do not currently use any analytics tools. If we add one, we'll update this policy.

## Cookies

The Tip Sheet itself does not set advertising cookies. If you follow a link to another website (for example Pinterest, or a source we cite), that site may set its own cookies, which are governed by its own privacy policy. Saved looks are stored only in your own browser on your device; we never receive them.

## Third-party links

Our articles link to other websites. We're not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

## Pinterest

If you use the "Save to Pinterest" button, you'll be taken to Pinterest, which has its own privacy policy.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete any personal information you've sent us. Depending on where you live (for example, California), local privacy laws may give you additional rights. To make a request, [contact us](/contact/).

Questions about this policy? [Contact us](/contact/).
