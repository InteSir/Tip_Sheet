**The Tip Sheet currently has no affiliate links.** We haven't joined any affiliate program, we don't recommend or link to specific products, and no link on this site earns us a commission.

## If that changes

We may join affiliate programs in the future. If we do:

- We'll name each program on this page and update the date above.
- Pages that contain affiliate links will carry a short note near the top saying so.
- Affiliate links will be marked as sponsored links in the page code, as search engines ask.
- A commission will never change the price you pay, and it won't decide which looks or advice we publish. Our [editorial standards](/editorial-standards/) explain how we work.

## Sponsored content

We don't publish sponsored content. If we ever do, or if we receive a free product, we'll say so clearly at the top of that article.

## Images

Nail images captioned "AI-generated inspiration" are illustrations of a design idea, not photos of any product. See [how we use AI images](/how-we-use-ai-images/).
