A good press-on manicure is mostly about the ten minutes of prep before you apply anything. Get that right and your set will look neater and stay on far longer. Here's the step-by-step routine.

## Step by step

1. **Size every nail first.** Lay out a press-on for each finger before opening the glue. Each one should cover the nail edge to edge without touching the skin. Between sizes? Follow your brand's advice first; otherwise size up and file thicker, filable tips, or size down with thin, flexible pre-glued tips. Our [sizing guide](/press-on-nails/sizing/) explains why.
2. **Push back cuticles.** Use a wooden or soft cuticle pusher. Press-ons that sit on cuticle skin lift early.
3. **Lightly buff the shine off.** A gentle buff gives the adhesive something to grip. Don't over-file.
4. **Remove every trace of oil.** Wipe each nail with an alcohol prep pad and don't touch your face or hair afterwards. This is the step that makes the biggest difference.
5. **Apply glue or a tab.** For glue, use a thin layer on your nail *and* a small drop on the press-on. For tabs, peel and press firmly onto the natural nail.
6. **Press at an angle and hold.** Start at the cuticle and roll down to the tip, then hold firm pressure for about 30 seconds per nail.
7. **Shape the finish.** File gently in one direction to adjust length or shape.

## Aftercare tips

- Avoid hot water, baths and dishwashing for the first hour or two.
- Use your finger pads, not your nail tips, to open cans and peel stickers.
- Wear gloves for cleaning.
- Keep a spare nail and a little glue in your bag for quick fixes.

## Removing press-ons safely

Soak your fingertips in warm, soapy water or a little oil for 10 to 15 minutes. Then gently lift from the sides with a cuticle stick and soak again if anything resists. Finish with cuticle oil.

> Prep is the manicure. The press-on is just the finish.
