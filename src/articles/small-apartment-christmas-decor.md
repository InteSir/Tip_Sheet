faq:
  - q: How do I decorate a small apartment for Christmas without clutter?
    a: Choose one focal point (a window, a mantel or a tree corner) and keep everything else to a few small touches. Stick to two or three colors.
  - q: What size Christmas tree fits a small apartment?
    a: Tabletop trees and slim pencil trees take up the least floor space. Measure your corner and ceiling height before you buy.
  - q: How can renters hang Christmas decor without damage?
    a: Use removable adhesive hooks and strips, tension rods in windows, and over-the-door hooks. Always check the weight rating.
