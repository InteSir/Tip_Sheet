Sizing is the step most people rush, and it's behind a surprising share of press-on problems: lifting at the sides, nails that pop up the moment you press them down, and sides that ache by the end of the day. Questions about wide beds, flat beds, tiny pinkies and "I'm between two sizes" come up constantly in press-on communities, and the answers online often contradict each other.

This guide keeps it simple. Measure each nail once in millimeters, write it on the printable chart, and then match by width against the actual tips in your box. Size numbers aren't standard from brand to brand (wearers report inconsistent sizing even between packs of the same line), so your measurements in millimeters are more useful than any size number.

## The in-between rule (and why sources disagree)

If you fall between two sizes, you'll find two schools of advice. Many handmade and gel-tip brands say **size up and file** the sidewalls down. KISS imPRESS says **size down**, not up.

<div class="callout"><p><strong>Why advice conflicts:</strong> the split seems to follow the type of tip. Thicker, filable or soft-gel tips can be filed to fit, so sizing up works. Thin, flexible, pre-glued tips that overhang your sidewalls end up touching skin and lifting, so sizing down works better. This is our reading of the sources, not a tested rule. Follow your brand's own sizing advice first. If it gives none: <strong>filable tips, size up and file; flexible pre-glued tips, size down.</strong></p></div>

Whichever way you go, don't force a tip that's too small flat onto your nail. That tension is what makes it spring off.

## Width is only half of fit

A tip can be the right width and still lift if its curve doesn't match yours. Too curved for a flat nail, and the edges press in and lift; too flat for a curved nail, and you get gaps along the sides. The nail-bed notes on this page help you spot which one you have and what to try.

Once your sizes are sorted, run through the [Press-On Prep Checklist](/press-on-nails/prep-checklist/), and if something still goes wrong, the [Press-On Problem Solver](/press-on-nails/problem-solver/) can help you work out why.
