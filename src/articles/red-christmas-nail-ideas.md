faq:
  - q: What shade of red is best for Christmas nails?
    a: Blue-based reds (cherry, crimson) feel the most classic and festive. Warmer orange-reds look brighter and more casual. Deep wine and burgundy reds work well if you want your nails to carry through January.
  - q: Are press-on nails a good option for Christmas nail art?
    a: Yes. Detailed designs like bows, stripes and foil accents are often easier to get as a press-on set than to paint yourself, and you can swap them for a New Year's look the following week.
  - q: Which nail shape suits red nails?
    a: Almond and oval shapes make red nails look elegant and lengthen the fingers. Short squoval or square shapes look neat and modern, especially for bold color mixes.
  - q: How long before Christmas should I get my nails done?
    a: If you are wearing press-ons, apply them one to two days before your main event so they have settled. See our guide on making press-on nails last for prep tips.
