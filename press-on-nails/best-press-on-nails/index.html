How to choose press-on nails

ConsiderWhat it means for you
ShapeAlmond and oval lengthen fingers; squoval and round are the most practical; square and coffin show off nail art.
LengthShort is easiest for typing and everyday tasks. Medium gives room for detail. Long is for statement looks.
FinishGlossy is classic, chrome and cat eye add shine, and matte looks modern but shows wear sooner.
FitMore sizes in the box means a better fit. A set with 24 or more nails usually covers most hands.
AdhesiveGlue for longer wear, adhesive tabs for a few days or an event.

FAQ

How do I know which press-on size to use?

Most sets include several sizes. Match each nail before you apply anything: the press-on should cover the nail from side wall to side wall without touching the skin. If you're between sizes, choose the smaller one and file the sides slightly.

Glue or adhesive tabs: which is better?

Nail glue usually holds longer, often around one to two weeks with good prep. Adhesive tabs are gentler and easier to remove, and suit wearing a set for a few days or an event. Many kits include both.

Can press-on nails be reused?

Often, yes, especially if you used adhesive tabs. Remove them gently, clean off leftover adhesive and store them in their original tray.

Do press-on nails damage natural nails?

Damage usually comes from prying them off. Soak them in warm, soapy water or oil and lift slowly from the sides. See our guide on making press-on nails last.
