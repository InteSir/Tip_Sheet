You don't need a big living room to feel festive. These small-space Christmas decor ideas focus on one or two high-impact moments, so your apartment feels cozy rather than crowded.

FAQ

How do I decorate a small apartment for Christmas without clutter?

Choose one focal point (a window, a mantel or a tree corner) and keep everything else to a few small touches. Stick to two or three colours.

What size Christmas tree fits a small apartment?

Tabletop trees and slim pencil trees take up the least floor space. Measure your corner and ceiling height before you buy.

How can renters hang Christmas decor without damage?

Use removable adhesive hooks and strips, tension rods in windows, and over-the-door hooks. Always check the weight rating.
